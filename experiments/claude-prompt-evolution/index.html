Claude Opus System Prompt Evolution

Word count by category — 4 Opus versions, May 2025 to April 2026

Prompts sourced from simonw/research (mirrors of Anthropic's published system prompts). Analysis informed by Simon Willison's May 2025 and April 2026 breakdowns.

The question: As models improve, do system prompts get shorter? The data shows prompts more than doubled in 11 months — but not all sections grew equally. Behavioral workarounds were retired into training; safety and agentic scaffolding expanded to fill the gap.
⚠ These are the claude.ai published system prompts, not the full API context. Tool descriptions (web search, Artifacts, computer use) are injected separately and are not counted here. Word counts are per-section; ~20–80 words of XML tag boilerplate are unassigned.

Key Findings

False

Prompts shrink as models improve

The opposite: 1,714 words in May 2025 → 3,686 words by April 2026. A 115% increase in 11 months.

True

Behavioral workarounds get retired

The behavior section grew (+202%) but its composition changed — explicit patches (letter-counting, puzzle constraints) were removed; new agentic guidance replaced them.

True

Safety is the fastest-growing category

447 → 1,315 words (+194%). Opus 4.7 added a dedicated child safety XML block, disordered eating guidance, and means restriction language.

Stable

Identity stays constant

479 → 540 words across all 4 versions. Who Claude is and what Anthropic is barely changed — those are trained in, not prompted.

Surprising

Soul document moved to training

Claude 4.5's 14,000-token "soul overview" values document was incorporated into the training run rather than the system prompt — baking values in instead of specifying them at runtime.

True

Linguistic hedges moved to training

Opus 4.7 dropped "genuinely," "honestly," and pushy follow-up language from the prompt. Tone calibration is now a training property, not a runtime instruction.
